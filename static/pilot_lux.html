Pilot 1.

LUXEMBOURG

Exploring EPH retrofit strategies in a single-family building to balance comfort, energy efficiency, and real-world feasibility.

See All Results

| About Pilot Area

Betzdorf, Luxembourg

In Luxembourg, the POSIFIT project is being demonstrated through innovative renovation strategies and smart technologies that transform existing residential buildings into energy positive homes, in collaboration with Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology

Passive Solutions

  • Envelope Improvements:
  • Wall Performance Improvement
  • Roof Performance Improvement
  • Window Performance Improvement
  • Window SHGC Improvement

Active Solutions

  • Photovoltaics
  • Heat Pump

Pilot in Numbers

Total Floor Area

The pilot covers a total floor area of approximately 250 square meters across multiple buildings.

Number of Housing Units

A total of 20 residential units will be renovated using the POSIFIT approach.

Energy Efficiency Goal

All units aim to become energy-positive through chosen solutions.

Duration of Renovation

The pilot implementation and renovation are expected to be completed over an 18-month timeline.

CO2 Reduction Target

The project targets a reduction of more than 60% in operational carbon emissions per household.

& Diagrams

  • U-Value Window: The U-value of a window indicates how much heat passes through it, measured in W/m²·K.
  • U-Value Roof: The U-value of a roof measures its heat loss rate, in W/m²·K.
  • U-Value Wall: The U-value indicates how much heat a wall loses, measured in W/m².
  • Heat Pump: A heat pump is an HVAC system component that transfers heat from sources like air, ground, or water to provide heating or cooling.
  • PV System: Photovoltaic (PV) panels convert solar radiation into electrical energy using semiconductor materials.
  • EV Charger: An electric vehicle charging station delivers DC or AC to recharge EV batteries.

Photos from the Pilot Visit

During the 4th Grand Assembly, the POSIFIT consortium visited the Luxembourg pilot site to gain hands-on insights into the construction process, material choices, retrofit approach, and architectural design strategies.
